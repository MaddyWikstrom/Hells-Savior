The Story

My name is Hell's Savior, a 23-year-old Cape Verdean-American melodic rap artist born and raised in Wareham, Massachusetts. Inspired by artists like Juice WRLD, Lil Uzi Vert, and Trippie Redd, my music is mainly based off real-life experiences, heartbreak, struggle, and whatever emotions I'm currently feeling.

The name Hell's Savior comes from the idea of duality — the belief that people are often judged by predetermined ideas instead of who they truly are or what they believe in. People are quick to place labels on things without ever taking the time to understand them.

Growing up, I was shy and often bullied for rapping. For years, I hid my music from people. Over time, I found my love for fashion, music, and self-expression and eventually learned to embrace who I am.

I grew up in a religious household, which gave me a unique perspective on life. Despite growing up around difficult situations, I've never smoked or drank in my life.

My first song I ever recorded was called NØ MØRE, and it earned over 2k streams on SoundCloud and helped launch my music career. Today, I record, mix, and master my own music and have built an audience of over 40k followers on TikTok, 11k followers on Instagram, and more than 1k monthly Spotify listeners. I'm widely known for my viral freestyle videos across social media.

I've performed in multiple states across the country and have opened for artists such as Kyle Richh and J.I the Prince of N.Y. Along the way, I've received support from Ski Mask The Slump God, Polo G, Dave East, Che, DDG, Freddie Dredd, DC The Don, Zaytoven, and many others.

Outside of music, I fuck with comic books, anime, jewelry, gothic culture, and movies. I'm also a fan of Edgar Allan Poe and the darker themes found in his work. My style is heavily influenced by gothic art, rock culture, and dark storytelling. One of my favorite movies is Coraline, and my celebrity crush is Jenna Ortega.

I work closely with my manager, Tsu9mii, who's got ten plus years in the game and has helped me cultivate everything I've achieved so far.

Love,
Hell's Savior — The Bat Boy • The Dark Cowboy aka the White Hoe Slayer
